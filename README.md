# Standard Operating Procedures

A portfolio collection demonstrating the design and documentation of structured Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for controlled business and technical processes.

## What's included

### Electronic Signature Process

An adapted portfolio sample based on an enterprise SOP covering the use of electronic signatures and the migration of documents between handwritten and electronic signature formats.

The SOP demonstrates:

* Purpose and scope definition
* In-scope and out-of-scope criteria
* Roles and responsibilities
* Document control and version history
* Standard terminology and conventions
* Step-by-step procedures
* Alternative process scenarios
* Verification and approval requirements
* Controls and monitoring
* References and supporting information

The original SOP uses a structured format with a formal document history, table of contents, defined roles, procedural sections and multiple process scenarios.

## Documentation approach

The samples demonstrate how complex business processes can be transformed into controlled, structured procedures that are clear for the people who need to execute them.

Key principles include:

* Clear purpose and scope
* Defined ownership and responsibilities
* Consistent terminology
* Numbered procedural steps
* Explicit conditions and exceptions
* Traceable document control
* Clear verification and approval points

## My role

Technical writing, process documentation, information architecture, content structure, procedural design and document control.

## Portfolio note

The samples are adapted for portfolio purposes. Company-specific names, identifiers, branding, internal systems and other proprietary details are removed or generalised where appropriate.

The original material represented a controlled enterprise SOP with formal approval and document-management requirements.
